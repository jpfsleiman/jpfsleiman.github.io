What I work on

I serve in the office of Representative Paul D. Tonko (NY-20) as one of about 30 Congressional Fellows nationwide in the AAAS Science & Technology Policy Fellowships. My portfolio covers:

  • Energy
  • Water and natural resources
  • National parks and cultural preservation
  • Museums and libraries
  • Critical minerals and battery recycling
  • Tribal issues
